# E21 — Engagement at range: does single-camera measurement work for sword fighting?

**Objective.** Much of human engagement happens at range: facing off, managing distance. Does the pipeline measure
the distance between two people, who is nearer, and whether they touch, in Harmony4D's sword fighting?

**Setup.** Harmony4D `05_sword2` test sequences (new people, never trained on), the unchanged pipeline. Criteria fixed
in advance: [`PREREG.md`](PREREG.md).

**Status.** Pre-registered 2026-10-27; results follow in `RESULTS.md`.
